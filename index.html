About the event

It's more than food. It's an experience.

Plantain Boy Experience is a rave experience built around the love of good food, good music and good vibes. Think a food festival that turns into a party: dozens of vendors, smoky grills, fresh roasted plantain, cold drinks and a stage that doesn't stop.

Come hungry, come dressed to dance, and come with your people. Our taste, our culture, one big night.

When and where is it?

How do I get my ticket?

Choose a ticket, fill in your details and pay securely with Paystack. Your ticket with a QR code arrives instantly by email from BLAKMIXED ENT.

Can I buy for my friends?

Yes — choose the quantity at checkout. You'll get one QR code per person.

Are tickets refundable?

Tickets are non-refundable but you can transfer yours to a friend by forwarding the email.
